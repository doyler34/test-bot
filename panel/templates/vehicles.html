Limits by category

With Helicopters at 2, each side can have at most 2 live helicopters at once, all of them together (a vehicle's variants count as the vehicle). Empty is unlimited, 0 allows none. A vehicle counts for the side of the player who placed it, or of the depot that sold it, whoever drives it; wrecked or deleted ones stop counting. Game Master vehicles don't count, except that when the server starts every live vehicle already in the world counts once for its own side. Lowering a limit never removes vehicles.
