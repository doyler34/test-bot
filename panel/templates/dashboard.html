Welcome to OYB Control, {{ user.username }}

A quick look round before you start: it takes under a minute. You can skip it, and run it again any time from the Guide.

Every server

A card for each server: online or not, players on, the map and ping. A red No admin on means nobody's watching it. ClickTap a card to open that server underneath, and the ★ keeps your favourites first.

The server you picked

Its join address, with a copy and Join button. Players opens its full player list and live feed{% if can("kick") %}, where you {{ "kick and ban" if can("ban") else "kick" }} people{% endif %}. Full page → has its history, health and the Monitor.

Live map

Every kill in the last 30 minutes, where it happened. Orange is a kill, red a teamkill, purple a kill by AI or nobody. Hover overTap a dot for who, what and from how far.

Players and the live feed

Switch this box between who's on now and the live feed: kills, teamkills, joins and Sus flags as they happen. ClickTap any name to open that player.

Getting around

The menu on the left has Players, Bans and everything else you have access to.The bar at the bottom has the dashboard, Players and Bans; More has everything else. You only ever see what you're allowed to use.

Search

Type a player's name, an old name or their ID to find them. A server's name takes you to that server.

The bell

A red number means something needs someone: a server down, lots of players and no admin on, or Sus flags in the last hour.

The guide

Everything in more detail, with pictures, under SupportMore → Guide. It only shows what your role can do. Leave a note on a player whenever you deal with them, and you're set.
