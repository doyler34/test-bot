When the weekly board resets

Every Monday at midnight Eastern the weekly leaderboard starts again, and the bot posts last week's top 3. It goes in the channel picked above, or the weekly leaderboard channel if none is picked. Nobody is pinged.

Wording

{week} becomes the date the week started, like 28 Sep. The top 3, with their kills, always go in the middle. **bold** and new lines work like in Discord.
