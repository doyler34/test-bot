Questions

A server says 0 players. Is it down?

Not necessarily. Online · empty means the server is up with nobody on. Offline means we can't reach it right now.

Can I play with friends on other platforms?

Yes. PC, Xbox and PlayStation players share these servers.

How do I get on the leaderboard?

Link your game account in the Discord. An admin approves it, and your kills and deaths are tracked from then on.
