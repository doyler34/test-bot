How XP works

Time on the servers is the steady one. Fighting and chatting add to it.

  • 1 XPfor every {{ minutes_per_xp }} minutes you play on a {{ doc.name }} server
  • +5 XPfor each match you actually fought in. Idling in the lobby earns nothing.
  • +2 XPfor every enemy kill
  • −{{ teamkill }} XPfor every teamkill
  • +1 XPfor a message in the Discord, up to 10 a day
