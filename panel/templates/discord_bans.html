Ban card in tickets

When a banned player opens a ticket (Ticket King or any ticket bot), the bot posts their ban into it so staff can see what it's about.

The bot opens a private thread called 🔒 Ban info in the ticket, posts the card there and adds everyone with that role. The player isn't added and can't see it. The bot needs Create Private Threads in the ticket channels, and only works for tickets made as channels. If it can't make the thread, the card isn't posted at all.

Tickets made in that channel's category, or as threads under it, are watched. Tick any other categories tickets go in:
