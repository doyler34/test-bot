Mass teamkills

When one player teamkills this many people this quickly, the bot posts it in the staff channel straight away, with who did it, on which server, and who they killed.

Each player is reported at most once every 5 minutes, so one rampage is one alert. It also shows as a Sus line in the live feed.

Automatic kick or ban

Counted within the same number of seconds as above, even with the alert turned off. Only gunfire teamkills count here, since a mortar or grenade on teammates can be an accident; the alert still counts them all. Switch one on to use it. Admins are never kicked or banned automatically. Each one is posted in the staff channel, the live feed and the audit log.

AP mine spam

An AP mine kill shows in the log as an explosion to the foot, credited to whoever placed it, even from kilometres away or after they've left. When one player reaches this many in one game (enemies, teammates and after they've left all count), the bot posts it in the staff channel once, with who they killed.

A game runs until the server restarts. The Sus line in the live feed keeps counting for the rest of the game either way.

Automatic kick or ban

Counted over the whole game. Only teammates killed by their mines count here, since mining the enemy is fair play; the alert above still counts every mine kill, so you can see who's laying loads of them. A ban still happens if they've left, since their mines keep killing. Switch one on to use it. Admins are never kicked or banned automatically. Each one is posted in the staff channel, the live feed and the audit log.

Stacked loadouts

The supplies refund glitch shows up as a player stuffed with scopes or morphine. After each autosave the panel counts both in what every player's character is carrying, and in the arsenal loadout it spawned from (a loadout saved earlier, or on the other side, doesn't count). When someone has more than this, the bot posts it in the staff channel once per game, with how many they had.

Automatic kick or ban

Kick only: kicked each time a save still shows the loadout (at most every 5 minutes). Ban only: banned straight away. Both: kicked the first time, and banned if a save 5 minutes or more after the kick still has it. Someone who has already left can't be kicked, so with the ban on they're banned. FIA players only get the alert, never a kick or ban, since they pick kit up off the field; check whether they hand it in for supplies. Admins are never kicked or banned automatically. Each one is posted in the staff channel, the live feed and the audit log.

Exploit checks

After each autosave the panel compares it with the one before. Each check posts in the staff channel and the live feed once a game per player, storage or base. They only alert for now: nothing here kicks or bans, so check before acting. Saves come anywhere from under a minute to ten minutes apart.

XP farming

A player whose game XP goes up by at least this much between two autosaves without a single kill. Capturing a base gives a lot at once, so keep this high.

Supply jumps and transfers

One storage gaining at least this many supplies between two autosaves. A jump is when the supplies on the map went up by that much overall, so they came from nowhere. A transfer is when another storage lost about the same amount and no truck's load changed.

The map's own depots don't have a position in the save, so those alerts can't say which base.

Base griefing

At least this many structures built by their own side disappearing between two autosaves with the same player nearby (within 75 m). The builder taking down their own doesn't count.

Enemies blowing things up won't trigger it, since they're on the other side. Check the GM log for who deleted them.

Vehicle griefing

A player running over this many teammates in one game, vehicles wrecked at their own side's main base, or one player taking out this many vehicles in a game.

Mines in own base

At least this many mines of a side inside a base that side holds (within 75 m of its HQ), where its own players walk. Mines further out may be defending it.

The save doesn't say who placed a mine, so this names the base, not a player.

Teleporting and under the map

A player on foot in both autosaves, on the same life, who moved at least this far between them, or a player on foot below sea level.

A vehicle ride in between can look like a teleport, so this one needs checking.

Ban evasion

Someone joining with the same Steam ID or BattlEye GUID as an account that's banned. Same-IP accounts are already banned automatically.
