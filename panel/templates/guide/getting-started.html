OYB Control runs in your web browser, on a phone or a computer. There's nothing to install.

Logging in

  1. Open the panel's address. An owner will send it to you along with your username and a temporary password.
  2. Type your username and password and press Log in.
  3. The first time, it asks you to pick your own password. Make it at least 10 characters and don't reuse one from anywhere else.
{{ shot("login", "The login page.") }}

Five wrong passwords in a row locks that account for 15 minutes. If you've forgotten yours, ask an owner to reset it rather than guessing.

The home page

After logging in you land on the home page. The top shows who you're logged in as and a few numbers at a glance: how many servers are up, how many players are on, active bans, and any Sus flags from the last day. Red there means something is worth a look.

Below that is a card for every server: online or not, players on, and memory. Click a card to open that server.

{{ shot("home", "The home page, with the welcome box and a card for each server.") }}

Your role

Everyone has their own login, and every action is written to the audit log with your name on it. What you can do depends on your role:

RoleCan
ModeratorSee the servers and players, kick, and leave notes on players.
AdminEverything a moderator can, plus ban and unban, see IPs and alt accounts, start, stop and restart servers, download and upload game logs, and read the audit log.
OwnerEverything, plus the raw RCON console and making admin accounts.

You're {{ "an" if user.role in ("admin", "owner") else "a" }} {{ user.role }}. Some pages in this guide cover things only admins or owners can do, and say so.

Your password

  1. Click your name in the top right.
  2. Type your current password, then the new one twice, and save.

Always log out on a shared computer: Log out is in the top right.
