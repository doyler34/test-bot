OYB Control runs in your web browser, on a phone or a computer. There's nothing to install. This guide shows the computerphone version because that's what you're reading it on; open it on the other and it changes to match.

Logging in

  1. Open the panel's address. A senior admin will send it to you along with your username and a temporary password.
  2. Type your username and password and press Log in.
  3. The first time, it asks you to pick your own password. Make it at least 10 characters and don't reuse one from anywhere else.
{{ shot("login", "The login page.") }}

Five wrong passwords in a row locks that account for 15 minutes. If you've forgotten yours, ask a senior admin to reset it rather than guessing.

Add it to your home screen (Share → Add to Home Screen on iPhone, ⋮ → Add to Home screen on Android) and it opens like an app.

Finding your way round

The first time you log in, a quick tour shows you round the dashboard. Take it again any time.

The menu down the left side has everything:

  • Dashboard: every server at a glance. See The dashboard.
  • Servers: each server by name, with a green dot when it's up. Click one to open it on the dashboard.
  • Players and Bans.
  • Data, Discord and Settings, if you have access to any of them.
  • Support: this guide.
{{ shot("sidebar", "The menu on the left.") }}

The bar along the bottom of the screen has Dashboard, Players and Bans. Tap More for everything else: Data, Discord and Settings if you have access to them, this guide, your account and Log out.

{{ shot("dock", "The bar at the bottom, with More open.") }}

You only ever see what you're allowed to use. If you need something that isn't there, ask a senior admin: they can switch single things on for you.

The top bar

  • Search: type a player's name, an old name or an ID and press Enter to find them. Type a server's name to jump to that server.
  • The bell: a red number means something needs someone now: a server offline or not reachable from outside, lots of players and no admin on, memory that didn't clear, Sus flags in the last hour{% if can("ips") %}, or a login attack{% endif %}. ClickTap it to see the list, and a line to go straight there.
  • Your name: your account (change your password here), the guide, and Log out.

Your role

Everyone has their own login, and every action is written to the audit log with your name on it. What you can do starts from your role:

RoleCan
ModeratorSee the servers and players (live feed, kill map and kill logs included), kick, and leave notes on players.
AdminEverything a moderator can, plus ban.
Senior adminUnbanning, IPs and alt accounts, starting and stopping servers, the Box dashboard, Discord, the website, settings and admin accounts.
ManagerEverything a senior admin can, plus the Server manager and the audit log.

A senior admin can also switch single things on or off for one person, like letting a moderator see the Box dashboard, or an admin who doesn't ban. You're {{ "an" if user.role == "admin" else "a" }} {{ user.role|rolename }}. This guide only shows you the pages for what you can do.

Your password

  1. Click your name in the top right, then Account.Tap More, then Account.
  2. Type your current password, then the new one twice, and save.
