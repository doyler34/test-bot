Senior admins manage who can log in from Settings → Admins.

{{ shot("users", "The Admins page.") }}

Adding someone

  1. Under New admin, type their username and pick a role: moderator, admin or senior admin.
  2. Press Create account. The panel shows a temporary password once.
  3. Send them the panel's address, their username and that password. They'll pick their own password the first time they log in.

Changing someone

  • Role: pick a new one from the list next to their name. It changes straight away.
  • Reset password: gives them a new temporary password, shown once. Use it when someone's locked out.
  • Disable: stops them logging in, without losing their history in the audit log.

Choosing what one person can do

A role is a starting point. For each person you can switch any single thing on or off, so nobody needs to be made senior admin just to do one job: say Blitz should see the Box dashboard but not unban, or an admin who shouldn't ban.

  1. Press Edit access next to their name, in the Access column.
  2. Everything they can do is ticked. What comes with their role is marked comes with admin (or moderator).
  3. Tick anything extra, or untick something to take it off them, even if their role normally has it.
  4. Press Save access. It applies on their next page load.
{{ shot("access", "One person's access: what they can do is ticked.") }}

The things you can switch: kicking, notes, banning, unbanning, changing any ban, IPs and login attacks, the audit log, starting and stopping servers, the Box dashboard and bot data, the server cards, the website, in-game admins, the Sus settings, the mortar calculator, and every Discord page or just some of them.

The Access column then says what's different for them, like 1 extra, 1 taken off, or Role only. Their menu and this guide change to match: they only see the pages they can use. Making accounts stays with senior admins, and senior admins always have everything.

Give people the lowest role that does the job, then switch on the odd extra. Start new staff as moderators.
