Owners manage who can log in, from Admins in the top menu.

{{ shot("users", "The Admins page.") }}

Adding someone

  1. Under New admin, type their username and pick a role: moderator, admin or owner.
  2. Press Create account. The panel shows a temporary password once.
  3. Send them the panel's address, their username and that password. They'll pick their own password the first time they log in.

Changing someone

  • Role: pick a new one from the list next to their name. It changes straight away.
  • Reset password: gives them a new temporary password, shown once. Use it when someone's locked out.
  • Disable: stops them logging in, without losing their history in the audit log.

Extra access for one person

Sometimes one person needs one thing their role doesn't give, like editing the Start here message without being an owner. In the Access column (it says Role only until they have something extra):

  1. Press Edit access next to their name.
  2. Tick what they should have: banning, unbanning, IPs, the audit log, the console, server power, the website, every Discord page, or only some Discord pages.
  3. Press Save access. It applies on their next page load.

Their menu and this guide change to match: they only see the pages they can use, and the guide pages that explain them. Owners always have everything, and only owners can change anyone's access.

Give people the lowest role that does the job, and add single things with Access rather than making them owner. Start new staff as moderators.
