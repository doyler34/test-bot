The live feed shows what's happening in game, newest at the top, and updates on its own every few seconds. It's in two places: the Live feed side of the box next to the map on the dashboard, and the Live feed tab on a server's own page.

What's in it

LabelMeans
Join / LeaveSomeone connected or left.{% if can("ips") %} Joins show the IP they came from.{% endif %}
SideSomeone picked a faction.
KillWho killed who and from how far. If it wasn't a bullet it says so, e.g. "fragmentation" or "explosive".
TKA teamkill. Someone who teamkills several players in a short time is also posted to the staff alerts channel in Discord.
SusSomething that looks like cheating. See Spotting cheaters.
AdminSomething an admin did in the panel: a kick, a ban, a restart.
Server / RCONThe server going up or down, and messages the game sends the panel.

Side tags

A small tag sits before each player's name in the joins, leaves, side picks and kills, so you can see who is on which side at a glance.

TagMeans
US (blue)Last picked the US side.
RU (red)Last picked the Russian side.
FIA (green)Last picked FIA.
? (pink)Hasn't picked a side that the panel has seen, or picked civilian.

Filtering

  1. ClickTap a chip at the top of the feed: Joins, Kills, TKs, Sus, Admin or RCON. TKs is just the teamkills; Kills has them mixed in with everything else.
  2. Only those rows stay. The chip you picked is lit up.
  3. All shows everything again.
{{ shot("feed-sus", "Filtered to Sus: just the flags.") }}

Each chip looks back through the whole 7 days, so picking Sus or TKs still finds older ones on a busy server.

The kill map

The Live Map on the dashboard and on a server's Overview puts kills where they happened. An orange dot is a kill, red a teamkill, purple a kill by AI or with nobody named, and the dashed line goes back to where the shot came from.

  • How far back: the picker next to the title goes from the last 30 minutes to the last 30 days. The last 30 minutes and hour update on their own.
  • Details: clicktap a dot for who killed who (both names open their player pages), how, from how far, when, and its grid reference.
  • Grid references: point at the map and the corner shows the grid reference under the pointer, the same 100 m squares as in game.every dot's details give its grid reference, the same 100 m squares as in game.
  • Layers on the map: pick another server, turn the killer-to-victim Lines off, show Density (each 500 m square shaded by how many died there, with the count), the 1 km Grid, or Count: clicktap anywhere and it counts the kills within 100 m to 1 km of that spot, with how many were teamkills or by AI.
  • ⤢ opens the map full screen; Esc closes it.

Put the map next to the live feed (switch the box beside it to Live feed) and you can see a teamkill in the feed and where it happened at once. A cluster of purple dots nobody can explain is worth a look: that's how an explosion cheat shows. The game's logs don't say which weapon was used, only the kind of damage.

The feed keeps the last 7 days. Sus flags are kept for 180 days. For anything older, every game is saved in full under History.
