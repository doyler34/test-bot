Box dashboard

Everything the machine the servers run on is doing: CPU, load, memory, disk, temperature, network in and out, and lost packets, charted over the last hour up to 7 days. Pick the period at the top.

  • Overview: the numbers now, and what stands out.
  • Box and Servers: the charts, for the whole box and for each game server.
  • Network check: runs a full check of the box's network and gives you a report to send the host when players complain about lag.

Login attacks

Every failed login on the box's SSH, its AMP panels and this panel, read every 30 seconds. An address with lots of failures in a few minutes is posted to the staff channel in Discord, and shows in the bell.

  • Admin IPs: add your own address (it's filled in for you) so your typos are never counted or posted. A range like 86.40.0.0/16 covers a home connection whose address changes.
  • How well the box is guarded, the ports open to the internet, and who's been trying this week.

IP lookup

Type an IP address to see who it's registered to (the internet provider and country) and every account the panel has seen on it.
