The staff channel

OYB Control posts to a staff-only Discord channel, so you hear about things even when the panel isn't open:

  • bans, unbans and kicks, with who did them and why;
  • server restarts, crashes, and a server going offline or coming back;
  • memory that didn't clear after a mission restart;
  • Sus flags, with the names they point at.

Staff alerts

When one player teamkills several people in a short time (3 in a minute unless an owner changed it), the bot posts it straight away in the staff alerts channel: who did it, on which server, and who they killed. It can ping a role, like Admin. Each player is reported at most once every 5 minutes, so one rampage is one alert. It also shows as a Sus line in the live feed.

Linked accounts

Players link their game account to their Discord through the OYB bot. Anyone who has played on one of our servers can link. A linked player's Discord account shows on their player page.

If a banned player opens a support ticket, the bot posts their ban into the ticket (account, length, reason and when it ends), so you can see straight away what it's about.

A linked player who's banned gets a DM from the bot telling them why, for how long, and how to appeal, and a Banned · length role in the Discord while the ban lasts.
