A kick removes a player from the server right now. They can rejoin straight away, so use it for a warning or to sort out someone who's stuck. For anything more, ban.

  1. Open the server, on the Overview tab.
  2. Find the player in the Players box.
  3. Press Kick on their row, and confirm.
  4. Leave a note on their player page saying why.
{{ shot("players-pane", "The Kick button (outlined) on a player's row.") }}

The kick shows in the live feed and the audit log with your name, and is posted to the staff Discord channel.
