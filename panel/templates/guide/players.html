Everyone who has ever joined one of our servers is on the Players page, whether they're on now or not.

Finding a player

  1. Click Players in the top menu.
  2. Type part of their name. Old names work too, and so does their identity ID{% if can("ips") %} or an IP address{% endif %}.
  3. Click their name to open their page.
{{ shot("players-search", "Searching for \"rook\" finds both of his accounts.") }}

You can also click any name in a server's player list or in History.

A player's page

{{ shot("player", "A player's page.") }}
  • In-game record: time played, kills, deaths, K/D and teamkills, and the Discord account they linked.
  • History: first and last seen, and a Ban button{% if not can("ban") %} (admins){% endif %}.
  • Admin notes, the names they've used, their ban history, and any suspicious incidents they were online for.

Notes

Whenever you deal with someone, leave a note: what happened, and what you did. Every admin on every server sees it, so the next person doesn't start from scratch.

  1. On their page, type in the Admin notes box.
  2. Press Add note. It's saved with your name and the time.

Alt accounts and ban evaders

Players on PC, PlayStation and Xbox get a different ID on each, and anyone can make a second account. What ties them together is the IP address they connect from.

The Connections box on a player's page lists every IP they've played from, their BattlEye GUID, and every other account seen on the same IP, with a Banned tag on any that are banned.

{{ shot("player-connections", "Pte Rook and his PlayStation account, Rook_PS5, on the same IP.") }}

In a server's player list, a pill next to a name shows how many other accounts they have. It's red if one of them is banned: that's very likely someone dodging a ban.

People in the same house share an IP too. Two accounts on one IP isn't proof they're the same person. Check the names, times and notes first.
