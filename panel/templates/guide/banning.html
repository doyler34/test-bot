Banning needs an admin. If someone needs banning, kick them, leave a note on their player page saying what happened, and ask an admin. This page shows what they'll do.

{% endif %}

A ban works on every server at once. If the player is on, they're kicked straight away. A server that's offline picks the ban up as soon as it's back.

Banning a player

  1. Click Bans in the top menu.
  2. In Player, start typing their in-game name. A list drops down as you type: players who are on right now come first, and it shows their other names and when they were last seen.
  3. Click the right player. Their ID fills in under the box, so you know you've got the right one. You can paste an identity ID instead of typing a name.
  4. Pick a Length: 1 hour, 1 day, 7 days, 30 days or permanent.
  5. Write a Reason. Keep it short and clear: other admins will read it later.
  6. Press Ban on all servers.

You can also press Ban on a player's row in the server's player list, or on their player page. That fills the form in for you.

IP bans

Tick IP ban for cheaters and ban evaders. It also:

  • bans every other account seen on the same IPs: their PC, PlayStation and Xbox accounts, and any alts, with the same length;
  • bans and kicks any new account that joins from one of those IPs later.

In the demo, the banned cheater came back on a new account called Dreadful, from the same IP. The panel banned and kicked him on its own:

People in the same house share an IP, so an IP ban hits them too. Leave it unticked unless you mean it.

The ban list

Under the form is every active ban: who, why, how long is left, who did it, and whether each server has it yet (green) or is still waiting (red). IP marks IP bans. Click Show expired and removed for old ones.

Discord role

If the banned player has linked their Discord, the bot gives them a Banned · length role (e.g. "Banned · 7 days"), so everyone in the Discord can see it. It's taken off when the ban ends or is lifted.

Unbanning

  1. On the Bans page, press Unban next to the ban, and confirm.
  2. It's lifted on every server.

An IP ban is undone one account at a time: unban each of their accounts.
