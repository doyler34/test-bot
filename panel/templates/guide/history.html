Every game is saved when the server next restarts: its whole log folder, kept for good. You can pull up any day's games, see exactly who played and what happened, and re-check them for cheating.

Finding a game

  1. Open the server and click the History tab.
  2. It lists the last 7 days of games: when each started, how long it ran, players, kills, and any Sus flags.
  3. To look at another day, pick a date. Press Last 7 days to go back.
  4. Press Open on a game.

Downloading a log

At the bottom of a game's page, Download the whole log folder gives you a .tar.gz with the console, script and error logs, for sending on or digging through. Downloads are recorded in the audit log.

Adding an old game

For a game from before the panel was running, or a log someone sent you:

  1. On the History tab, scroll to Add an old game at the bottom.
  2. Choose the console.log file (it can be renamed, e.g. a .txt).
  3. Press Upload. It goes into History like any other game and opens its page.
