How members send it

Add a Give feedback button to a post, like one in your #feedback channel: Posts → New post, then a button that does Give feedback. Pressing it opens a short form: what it's about (picked from your topics, or typed if you haven't set any), and their feedback. It lands in the channel picked above with their name, and they can send one every 5 minutes.

What they're told

For staff

Each one has two buttons: Reply by DM sends them a message from the bot (it's noted on the feedback too), and Done turns it green and sends the DM above.
