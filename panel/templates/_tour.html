The tour takes about two minutes and walks you through the pages you'll use most.

Every server

A card for each server: online or not, players on, the map and ping. A red No admin on means nobody's watching it.

{{ tap|safe }} a card to open that server underneath. The ★ keeps your favourites first, just for you.

The server you picked

Its join address, with a copy and Join button. Players opens its full player list and live feed. Full page → has its history, health and the Monitor.

Server information

How long it's been up, who's on, and which admins are in game. Red None next to Admins on means you might want to jump in.

Live map

Every kill in the last 30 minutes, where it happened. a kill, a teamkill, a kill by AI or with nobody named. Hover overTap a dot for who, with what and from how far.

A cluster of purple nobody can explain is how an explosion cheat shows.

Players and the live feed

Switch this box between who's on now and the live feed. Next to the map, you can spot a teamkill in the feed and see where it happened at once. {{ tap|safe }} any name to open that player.

Charts

CPU, memory, players and network over the last hour. A sudden spike when players complain about lag is worth mentioning to a senior admin.

Getting around

The menu on the left has Players, Bans and everything else you have access to. The Servers list jumps straight to a server.The bar at the bottom has the dashboard, Players and Bans. More has everything else, your account and Log out.

Search

Type a player's name, an old name or their ID, and press Enter. A server's name takes you to that server.

The bell

A red number means something needs someone: a server down, lots of players and no admin on, or Sus flags in the last hour. {{ tap|safe }} it for the list.
